About

I studied UX/UI design at Cité des Métiers et des Compétences (CMC). During my internship at International School El Jadida, I took three mobile apps and a web app from user research and wireframes to a design system and full UI in Figma.

3D and voice are part of the same practice. I model and texture scenes in Blender, train interns on 3D basics, and hosted the two-day Arabic radio broadcast at the first CMC RSK forum in Rabat.
